Punchy, big, clean?

A short listening test for Playhead. About 8 minutes.

You will hear pairs of short impact sounds (booms, hits, cracks), each made of a few layered samples. For each pair, one question: which one is punchier, bigger or cleaner. There are no right answers; go with what you hear. Play each sound as often as you like.

  • Punchy: the hit lands hard and at once, tight and focused, with nothing soft or late around it.
  • Big: large and full, with weight in the low end, a wide spread of frequencies and a long body or tail.
  • Clean: one clear hit whose parts don't smear or cover each other; no flams, no mud.

Please use headphones or good speakers in a quiet room. The two sounds of a pair are equally loud, so loudness alone won't decide.

Nothing is sent from this page. At the end you save a small file with your answers (which sound you chose, how often you played each, how long you took, and the two questions below) and send it as described at the end. No name, no address, no tracking.
